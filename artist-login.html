ℹ️ How does it work?
📨 Step 1: Inquiry

A client has found your act and sends an inquiry. You will receive a notification in the Inquiries tab.

💬 Step 2: Contact the client

After receiving the inquiry, contact the client directly via WhatsApp or email to discuss all details.

✅ Step 3: Accept or decline

Accept or decline the inquiry in your Inquiries tab. The client will be notified automatically.
